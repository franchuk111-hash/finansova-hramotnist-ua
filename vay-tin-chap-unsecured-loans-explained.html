Vay tín chấp explained: unsecured loans in Vietnam without collateral

8 November 2026 · Vay24

What the term means

Vay tín chấp is a loan approved on income and credit history alone, with no collateral. In practice the lender verifies salary payments, your phone number and your repayment record with other institutions, which is why approval takes longer than a collateral loan.

Typical terms

Amounts from 10 to 200 million VND, terms from 6 to 36 months, and monthly rates that look small but compound into 25–45% per year. Always ask for the total amount payable, not the monthly rate — the two differ by a factor that surprises most first-time borrowers.

Who gets approved

Salaried employees with a labour contract and at least three months of salary transfers have the highest approval rates. Self-employed applicants usually need six months of bank statements and a higher down payment, and often a guarantor.

The rollover trap

If you cannot pay, some lenders offer to roll the loan into a new one. Each rollover adds fees and resets the term. Two rollovers usually cost more in interest than the original principal, and the debt stops shrinking.

Pay early if you can

Most unsecured contracts allow early repayment without a penalty, and interest is charged on the outstanding balance. Paying three months early on a 24-month loan can save a quarter of the total interest.

What lenders check first

Salary transfers into a bank account, the length of your phone contract, and your repayment record at other institutions. A three-month-old account with regular salary credits scores better than a five-year-old account with irregular deposits, because the model reads the pattern, not the age.

Insurance add-ons

Many contracts bundle credit insurance, which can add 3–8% to the total cost. It is sometimes optional and sometimes a condition of approval. Ask explicitly, and if it is optional, compare the price without it before signing.
