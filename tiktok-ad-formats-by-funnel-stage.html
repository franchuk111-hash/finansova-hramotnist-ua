TikTok ad formats: which one fits which funnel stage

Оновлено 4 November 2026 · Business Ads Guide

Cold reach: don't buy the expensive format

TopView and other takeover placements are priced for brands buying awareness at volume. For a business spending under $5,000 a month, that budget buys a day of impressions and no learning. Cold reach is better served by standard in-feed video with a hook in the first 1.5 seconds — the cheapest way to find out whether anyone cares.

Warm interest: Spark Ads do the heavy lifting

Spark Ads run through a creator's own account, so the ad inherits their profile, comments and existing engagement. In practice they outperform whitelisted reposts because viewers can tap through to a real person. This is the format to scale once you know which hook works — not the format to test with, since you need a creator relationship first.

Intent: Search Ads are underused

TikTok search behaves like a young YouTube: people type full questions. Search Ads put your video in front of users who already expressed intent, at a fraction of in-feed CPMs. Most advertisers ignore this placement, which is precisely why it still works. Budget 10–15% of spend here before scaling it.

Conversion: the format matters less than the landing page

At the bottom of the funnel, video format differences shrink and page speed dominates. A 1.8-second load beats a better creative with a 4-second load almost every time. If your conversion rate is stuck, fix the page before buying another format.
