Loans for foreigners in Vietnam: what lenders actually ask for

8 November 2026 · Vay24

Who can actually borrow

Most Vietnamese online lenders require a Vietnamese ID card (CCCD) and a local bank account. Foreigners without a CCCD are usually declined at the first step, regardless of income. The workable routes are banks with expat products, employer-backed salary advances, and a small number of apps that accept a passport plus a valid visa.

Documents that matter

Passport with a visa or temporary residence card, a Vietnamese phone number registered in your own name, a local bank account with at least three months of activity, and proof of income — either a labour contract or six months of bank statements.

What the extra risk costs

Rates for foreign applicants typically run 2–6 percentage points above the local rate, because the risk profile is harder to score. On a 20 million VND loan over 12 months, that difference is several million VND — enough to justify a week of paperwork at a bank.

The three most common rejections

Short account history, a phone number registered to someone else, and a visa with less than six months remaining. The first two you can fix in a month. The third you cannot fix, so plan the application around your visa renewal.

Avoid the brokers

Anyone who asks for an upfront fee to «guarantee approval» is not a lender. Legitimate institutions charge nothing before disbursement, and a broker cannot influence a credit score.

The salary-advance route

If your employer is a registered company, a salary advance is often the cheapest option available to a foreigner: no credit check, repayment deducted from the next payroll, and no impact on your credit file. Ask HR before applying anywhere else.

Building a file that scores

Six months of salary transfers into one Vietnamese account, a phone number in your own name, and a permanent residence card instead of a tourist visa will move you from declined to approved at local rates in most institutions. Start the account early — it is the single document you cannot rush.
