TikTok ads for local service businesses: radius, budget, creative

Оновлено 5 листопада 2026 · BusinessAdsGuide

Local service businesses — salons, repair crews, dental clinics, driving schools — are told TikTok does not work for them. It does, but the setup is different from e-commerce, and copying an e-commerce structure wastes most of the budget.

Radius first, creative second

Set a geo radius before you touch creative. A 5 km radius around a single location in a dense city reaches enough people; a 40 km radius in a rural area may still be too small. Check the estimated audience: below 80 000 people, the auction has too few impressions per day and frequency climbs fast.

Practical ranges that hold up:

  • Dense urban: 3-8 km.
  • Suburban: 10-15 km.
  • Regional town: 20-30 km, plus the next town if the service justifies the drive.

Budget shape

Local accounts rarely need more than 20-40 per day per location. Below 10 per day, the algorithm cannot exit learning. Above 50 per day in a small radius, you pay for the same people repeatedly.

Split budget 70/30: prospecting inside the radius, retargeting people who watched 50% or engaged. Local retargeting pools are small but convert 3-5x better than prospecting.

Creative that works locally

  • Show the actual location: street, storefront, sign. People need to recognize it.
  • Name the district or landmark in the first 3 seconds.
  • Show turnaround time ("same day", "within 24 hours") — this is the main decision factor locally.
  • Use a real employee, not stock footage. Local trust is face-based.

What fails: national-looking brand films, prices without context, and any creative that could belong to a business in another city.

The measurement trap

Local businesses often cannot track walk-ins. Use two proxies: a promo code unique to TikTok, and a question at booking ("where did you hear about us"). A/B testing landing pages is pointless if you cannot attribute the visit.
