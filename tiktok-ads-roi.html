TikTok Ads ROI: the four numbers that decide if a campaign survives

Оновлено 24 жовтня 2026 · Business Ads Guide

Most TikTok campaigns do not fail because the creative is bad. They fail because nobody defined the break-even point before spending. Four numbers settle that question in under ten minutes.

1. Contribution margin per sale

Not revenue — margin after product cost, payment fees and shipping. A $60 order with $24 of variable cost leaves $36. Every later calculation uses this number, so getting it wrong makes the whole model useless.

2. Cost per acquisition

Spend divided by attributed conversions. Attribution matters: TikTok's in-platform number usually overstates results compared with a post-purchase survey or a holdout test. Use the conservative figure when deciding whether to scale.

3. Break-even CPA

It is exactly the contribution margin. If margin is $36, a CPA of $36 is a zero-profit campaign. Most advertisers discover this after two weeks of spending, when the account has already learned the wrong audience.

4. Payback window

For subscription or repeat-purchase products, first-order CPA can exceed margin if the customer returns. Define the window — 30, 60, 90 days — and measure repeat rate inside it. Without that number, 'we will make it up on repeat' is a guess, not a plan.

The TikTok Ads ROI calculator runs all four inputs together and shows profit, break-even CPA and margin of safety, so a media buyer can see whether a campaign is viable before the first dollar is spent.
