TikTok Spark Ads: turning a creator post into paid media without reshooting

Оновлено 28 October 2026 · Business Ads Guide

Spark Ads let you promote a post that already lives on a creator's profile, keeping its likes, comments and shares attached to the paid delivery. For advertisers this solves the most expensive part of short-form video: the shoot. For creators it means the ad looks like their content because it is their content. The trade-off is control — and most accounts discover the limits of that control only after the campaign is live.

What you actually get

You get the post's existing engagement as social proof, its native caption and audio, and a CTA button you add on top. You do not get to edit the video, swap the caption copy freely, or guarantee the creator keeps the post live. That last point is the operational risk: if the creator deletes or archives the post, the ad stops. Always agree in writing that the post stays public for the full flight.

Where Spark Ads beat standard in-feed

Two cases. First, when the creative is a talking-head or testimonial that reads as an ad the moment you add a logo — the organic wrapper keeps it native. Second, when you are amplifying proven organic winners rather than testing new concepts: you already know the watch time, so paid budget buys distribution instead of validation. The mechanics of the format itself are covered in TikTok ad formats.

Authorization is the bottleneck

The creator generates a one-time code in their app, you paste it in Ads Manager, and the post becomes available to your ad account. Codes expire and are single-use, so build a two-day buffer before launch. For agency accounts, collect codes in one document with expiry timestamps — chasing a code at launch is the most common reason a Spark campaign slips a day.

Measurement you should set up first

Spark Ads inherit organic engagement, which pollutes naive click metrics: comments and profile visits inflate. Track view-through conversions and the deduplicated conversion window instead, and compare against a standard in-feed control rather than against the organic baseline. Setup order matters — if the pixel and events are not firing before launch, you will read the campaign as a failure when it is a measurement gap. End-to-end walkthrough: how to run TikTok ads.

Bottom line: Spark Ads are a distribution tool for creative you already trust, not a testing tool for creative you do not. Use them after the concept proves itself, and put the saved production budget into more variants.
