The TikTok Ads budget floor for small businesses (and why $50/day is not it)

Оновлено 25 October 2026 · Business Ads Guide

Most TikTok Ads guides quote a $50 daily minimum. The number is technically right and practically useless: $50/day for three days buys a few thousand impressions in a mid-cost market, which is not enough to separate a good creative from a bad one.

The number that matters

TikTok's delivery system needs roughly 50 optimisation events per ad group per week to leave the learning phase. At a $12 cost per conversion that is $600 per week — not $50 per day. Optimise for clicks at $0.40 and the entry point drops to about $20/day. The budget floor follows your conversion cost, not the platform minimum.

Three realistic tiers

$300–600 per month: one campaign, one ad group, click optimisation, traffic to a landing page — enough to learn whether the audience responds, not enough to optimise for purchases. $1,500–3,000: two ad groups, conversion optimisation, creative rotation every 7–10 days — the point where CPA becomes measurable. $5,000+: full funnel and retargeting; below that, retargeting audiences stay too small to deliver.

Where small budgets leak

Splitting $600 across four audiences gives each one $150 — below the learning threshold. Creative that has not changed in a month is a budget leak, because TikTok creative decays in 7–14 days. And purchase optimisation at $600/month produces almost no events; start with add-to-cart or a lead form.

The honest expectation

At $500/month in a competitive market, expect 150–400 landing page visits and a CTR between 0.8% and 1.8%. That is a test, not a channel. Accounts that treat month one as a creative test survive to month three, when the data becomes actionable.
